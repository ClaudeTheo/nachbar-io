# Nachbar.io Runway Prompts v2 - Enterprise Look

Diese v2-Bilder sind die bevorzugten Vorlagen. Die erste Version war eher Konzept/Storyboard; v2 ist fuer einen hochwertigeren Enterprise-App-Auftritt gedacht.

## 1. Nachbar.io allgemein

Bildvorlage:
`runway-refs-v2/nachbario-general-enterprise-reference.png`

Runway Video-Prompt:
```text
Create a premium 10 second enterprise app commercial from this exact reference image. Keep the phone screen, Nachbar.io logo, UI layout, colors and German text as stable and readable as possible. Use a slow cinematic push-in with subtle parallax in the dark neighborhood background, soft premium lighting, calm trustworthy movement, no fast cuts. The product must feel like a polished 2026 European civic-tech app, not a playful school project. No extra text, no new logos, no distorted UI, no flickering screen, no warped letters.
```
